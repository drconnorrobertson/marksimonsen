About

Built for the Mountain West.

Mark Simonsen is a Certified Public Accountant and Managing Partner of the Northwest Region at AE Tax Advisors. Based in Montana, Mark brings deep roots in the local business community and a sharp understanding of the tax challenges facing business owners and investors across the Mountain West.

With a career built on advanced tax strategy, Mark works with clients whose financial situations have outgrown standard compliance. His focus is on proactive planning that reduces tax liability, protects assets, and accelerates wealth building through real estate, entity structuring, and strategic positioning.

What Sets Mark Apart

Mark's approach combines the rigor of a CPA with the strategic thinking of an advisor who understands how business owners and investors actually build wealth. He does not simply prepare returns. He builds tax strategies that move the needle.

Services

Specialized Tax Strategy

Mark works with business owners, real estate investors, and entrepreneurs who need more than basic tax preparation. Every engagement is built around strategies that produce measurable results.

Entity Structuring

Strategic entity design for business owners and investors. S-Corp elections, holding company architecture, and multi-entity frameworks that minimize tax exposure while maintaining operational flexibility.

Real Estate Tax Planning

Comprehensive tax strategy for property investors. Depreciation optimization, 1031 exchanges, passive activity planning, and portfolio-level tax positioning for long-term and short-term rental portfolios.

Cost Segregation

Engineered cost segregation studies that reclassify building components into accelerated depreciation categories. Year-one deductions that dramatically reduce taxable income for commercial and residential property owners.

STR Strategy

Short-term rental tax optimization including material participation planning, the 7-day rule, bonus depreciation strategies, and structuring STR portfolios for maximum tax efficiency under current IRS guidelines.

Business Owner Optimization

Holistic tax strategy for business owners including reasonable compensation analysis, retirement plan design, income splitting, C-Corp election analysis, and multi-year strategic tax planning.

Prior-Year Amendments

Forensic review of prior-year tax returns to identify missed deductions, overlooked credits, and improperly classified assets. Amended returns that recover tax dollars left on the table.
