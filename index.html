About

Built for the Mountain West.

Mark Simonsen is a Certified Public Accountant and Managing Partner of the Northwest Region at AE Tax Advisors. Based in Montana, Mark brings deep roots in the local business community and a sharp understanding of the tax challenges facing business owners and investors across the Mountain West.

With a career built on advanced tax strategy, Mark works with clients whose financial situations have outgrown standard compliance. His focus is on proactive planning that reduces tax liability, protects assets, and accelerates wealth building through real estate, entity structuring, and strategic positioning.

"The difference between paying taxes and overpaying taxes is having someone in your corner who understands the code and knows where to look." Mark Simonsen, CPA
Services

Specialized Tax Strategy

Mark works with business owners, real estate investors, and entrepreneurs who need more than basic tax preparation. Every engagement is built around strategies that produce measurable results.

Entity Structuring

Strategic entity design for business owners and investors. S-Corp elections, holding company architecture, and multi-entity frameworks that minimize tax exposure.

Real Estate Tax Planning

Comprehensive tax strategy for property investors. Depreciation optimization, 1031 exchanges, passive activity planning, and portfolio-level positioning.

Cost Segregation

Engineered cost segregation studies that reclassify building components into accelerated depreciation categories for substantial year-one deductions.

Short-Term Rental Strategy

STR tax optimization including material participation planning, the 7-day rule, and bonus depreciation strategies under current IRS guidelines.

Business Owner Optimization

Holistic tax strategy including reasonable compensation analysis, retirement plan design, income splitting, and multi-year strategic planning.

Prior-Year Amendments

Forensic review of prior-year returns to identify missed deductions and credits. Amended returns that recover tax dollars left on the table.
