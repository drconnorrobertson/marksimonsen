CPA for Montana Business Owners | Advanced Tax Strategy for the Mountain West

Montana business owners operate in a unique tax environment. Without a state sales tax and with favorable property tax structures, the state attracts entrepreneurs, ranchers, real estate investors, and small business operators. Navigating federal tax obligations, entity selection, and depreciation strategies in Montana requires a tax advisor who understands both the local landscape and the broader regulatory framework.

Mark Simonsen, CPA is the Managing Partner of the Northwest Region at AE Tax Advisors. He works directly with Montana business owners, real estate investors, and entrepreneurs to build tax strategies that reduce liability, protect assets, and create long-term financial efficiency.

The Montana Business Tax Landscape

Montana stands apart from many states. There is no state sales tax, which simplifies compliance for retail and service businesses. The state corporate income tax rate applies to C corporations doing business in Montana, while pass-through entities like S corporations and LLCs pass income to their owners for taxation at the individual level.

For business owners with real estate holdings, Montana offers strong opportunities for depreciation acceleration through cost segregation studies. Ranch properties, commercial buildings, and short-term rentals across the state all carry significant reclassifiable assets that can produce substantial first-year tax savings.

Entity Structuring for Montana Businesses

One of the most impactful decisions a business owner can make is selecting the right entity type. The difference between operating as a sole proprietor, an LLC, an S corporation, or a C corporation can mean tens of thousands of dollars in annual tax savings.

Mark Simonsen and the AE Tax Advisors team evaluate each client's situation to determine the optimal structure:

  • Self-employment tax reduction through reasonable compensation planning in S corporations
  • QBI deduction optimization for pass-through entities under IRC Section 199A
  • C corporation strategies for businesses that benefit from the flat 21% corporate rate
  • Multi-entity structures that separate operating risk from real estate holdings
  • Retirement plan integration to maximize deductible contributions

Why Montana Business Owners Need Proactive Tax Strategy

Most business owners meet with their accountant once a year at tax time. By then, the year is closed and the opportunities have passed. Proactive tax planning identifies strategies before the year ends, giving the business owner time to act.

AE Tax Advisors takes a planning-first approach. Mark Simonsen begins with a comprehensive review of each client's financial situation, then builds a multi-year tax plan with specific strategies and implementation timelines.

Common strategies for Montana business owners include:

  • Cost segregation studies on commercial and rental properties
  • Entity restructuring to reduce self-employment tax
  • Retirement plan design including solo 401(k), SEP IRA, and defined benefit plans
  • Prior-year amendment recovery for missed deductions
  • Real estate professional status qualification
